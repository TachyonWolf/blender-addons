# Digit's VR

Pose characters and move, rotate and scale objects from inside VR. Needs Blender 5.3 or newer.

This add-on is in development.

Edit mode shows selected faces (orange) and the hovered face (blue) as see-through fills, and Blender's own axis marker shows where the laser starts, in front of your finger.

Select bubble: in Edit mode pick the Select tool, in Object or Pose mode turn on "Select bubble" in the Tools rows. Hold the trigger to paint a selection with a bubble at your fingertip, hold A to remove, hold B and move your hand up or down to resize it.
